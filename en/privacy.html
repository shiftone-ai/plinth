Privacy Policy

Plinth is a kiosk app for Mac, iPhone and iPad, provided by Shift One. This policy describes data handled by the app and by our support contact.

Data on your device

Exhibition settings, including the start URL and display preferences, stay in the app's local storage. The administrator password is stored as a salted verification value in the device Keychain. Plinth uses the operating system's biometric authentication and does not receive biometric data.

Your language choice stays on the device. On Mac, removing the app does not remove the profile in Application Support or the language preference in UserDefaults. Uninstalling the iPhone or iPad app removes its app container. Keychain items may remain after uninstall; a new administrator password replaces the existing verification value. Contact support for local removal steps.

Plinth writes limited operational diagnostics to the device's system log, such as state changes and error codes. It does not put your password, full exhibition URL or page content in these records. The operating system manages log retention.

Exhibition websites

Plinth loads the website chosen by an administrator. That website and any embedded services may receive network requests and process visitor data under their own policies. Plinth does not control them.

The app uses a temporary WebKit data store. The “New browsing session” setting starts a fresh store when the exhibition view is recreated. “Keep cookies and site data” reuses the store while the app runs. The store is discarded when the app runtime ends.

Web management on your network

Web management is off by default. If an administrator enables it on the device, Plinth accepts connections from browsers on the same local network. The browser sends the administrator password and setting changes to the device over unencrypted HTTP. Other people on that network may be able to read this traffic. Use a network separated from untrusted devices.

After sign-in, the browser keeps a temporary session token in the open page's memory. Plinth keeps the session on the device and clears it when web management is turned off. This connection does not send the settings or password to Shift One.

Data sent to us

The app does not send exhibition settings, credentials, diagnostics or analytics to Shift One. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives the normal connection data needed to serve them.

If you email support, we use your address and the information you provide to answer your request. Do not send passwords or private exhibition content.
